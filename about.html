OUR STORY

The Mofongo dish has roots that reach to the Dominican and Puerto Rican kitchens. Although it is now credited that this delicious dish originated in Puerto Rico, its deep African culinary lineage brought by the transatlantic routes to the Caribbean gives Mofongo a sacred reverence among food lovers.

We are La Casa del Mofongo, specializing in this delicious treat, sure to please the most selective palate! We are a family establishment, focusing on providing our guests with the best raw Caribbean and Latin kitchen has to offer. You will be delighted with our variety of dishes and unbelievable service.

Conveniently located on Georgia Avenue, in the heart of Silver Spring, Maryland, thank you for enjoying it!

Thank you for choosing La Casa del Mofongo!

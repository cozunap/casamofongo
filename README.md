# La Casa Del Mofongo - Silver Spring, MD

Official Multi-Page Coming Soon & Web Platform for **La Casa Del Mofongo** located in Silver Spring, Maryland, United States.

> **Description:** Homestyle Caribbean & Latin fare, such as mashed plantains & pork chops, in a simple space with TVs.  
> **Service Options:** Happy hour food · Great cocktails · Kids' menu  
> **Address:** 9441 Georgia Ave, Silver Spring, MD 20910, United States  
> **Phone:** [+1 240-863-3750](tel:+12408633750)  
> **Hours:** Open · Closes 10 p.m.

## Project Structure
- `index.html`: Hero with live countdown, VIP lead capture, service option highlights (Cocktails, Happy Hour, Kids' Menu, TV screens), and location preview.
- `menu.html`: Mashed plantains (Mofongo), seasoned pork chops (Chuletas), trifongo, happy hour food, cocktails, and kids' menu.
- `locations.html`: Detailed location card, interactive Google Maps directions, phone and opening hours.
- `contact.html`: Contact and reservation inquiry form, direct phone link, and service options breakdown.
- `css/styles.css`: Luxury design tokens (Deep Navy & Gold), typography (Playfair Display + Inter), and micro-animations.
- `js/components.js`: Dynamic bilingual navbar, sticky navigation, mobile hamburger drawer, shared footer, live countdown timer, and lead form handling.

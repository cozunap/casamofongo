# La Casa Del Mofongo MD - Official Website

Grand Restaurant-themed website for **La Casa Del Mofongo** located in Silver Spring, Maryland.

- **Theme & Architecture:** Inspired by [Grand Restaurant Theme](https://themes.themegoods.com/grandrestaurantv6/home-2/) with multi-page luxury design, rich crimson & gold accents, Playfair Display typography, responsive sticky header, and dedicated pages.
- **Pages:**
  - `index.html`: Home page with hero slider, welcome story, 3-photo atmosphere strip, red goals banner, online table booking form, interactive menu tabs, dish carousel, and interactive Google map.
  - `menu.html`: Full food & drink menu with featured main courses (Garlic Shrimp, Lobster tail with shrimp, Pollo guisado), signature specialties grid, side dishes, poultry & appetizers, and juices/cocktails.
  - `about.html`: Our Story (the origins of Mofongo & Caribbean hospitality), "Biscocho Gratis en tu Cumpleaños" with celebration combos (#1, #2, #3), and restaurant interior showcases.
  - `gallery.html`: "Que Hacemos" photo gallery featuring authentic Dominican & Latin dishes.
  - `contact.html`: Contact details, opening hours, phone callout, inquiry form, and embedded Google Maps.
